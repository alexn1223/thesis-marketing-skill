# Content calendar

Default: 2 weeks, 3-4 pieces a week. Ask only how many weeks and which platforms they use.

| Day | Platform | Format | Angle | Thesis point | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | LinkedIn | insight | … | key_points[0] | primary |
| Wed | X | thread | … | key_points[1] | question 1 |
| Fri | Telegram | story | … | edge | — |

Rules:
- Cover every key point once before repeating one.
- At least one piece a week answers a thesis `question`. These build SEO.
- If there is an article, schedule its distribution posts (see `seo.md`).
- Vary formats; never two threads in a row.
- Every row has the same CTA target: the thesis link.

Deliver as a table. Offer to write any row. Written rows go through `check_post.py` like any post.
