# The legal line

Fish Schools are private investment clubs. Public content must never offer an investment or ask anyone for money. Talking about markets, theses, companies and why a space matters is fine: analysis, not offers.

## Never write, in any wording

- invest with me / us, invest in my School or fund, back me
- join my / our / the / this fund, School, club, pool, syndicate or SPV
- I'm raising, we're fundraising, raising a fund, round or capital
- minimums, allocation, LP spots, spots left, last chance, closing soon, don't miss out, or any urgency to put money in
- return figures (10x, 25% APY, IRR), promises (guaranteed, risk-free, can't lose, passive income), price predictions
- fund terms: lockups, redemption windows, check sizes, fees, carry
- send / deposit USDC or any other asset
- "not financial advice" / "NFA" (it signals an offer)
- anything on the user's `not_for_posts` list

`scripts/check_post.py` blocks these. Treat its result as final: a blocked draft is never shown as ready.

## The call to action

Every organizer post ends with exactly one CTA. It is clear and direct, and it points to the **thesis**:

- "Full thesis: {link}"
- "I wrote up the whole thesis here: {link}"
- "If you're building or investing in this space, read the thesis and tell me where I'm wrong: {link}"
- "Following this theme closely. Thesis and updates: {link}"
- "DM me if you see it differently. Thesis: {link}"

No link → the CTA invites replies or DMs about the thesis. Instagram → "Thesis link in bio".

"Investing" as a topic is fine ("how investors should think about agent infra"). "Investing" as an offer is not.
