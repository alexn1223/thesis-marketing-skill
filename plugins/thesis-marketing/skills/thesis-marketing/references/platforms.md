# Platforms

| Platform | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X | 280 per post | Hook in the first line. 0-2 hashtags. Links count as 23 characters. Threads: 3-7 posts, CTA in the last. |
| LinkedIn | 3000 | First 2 lines must earn the "see more" click. Short paragraphs, white space. Up to 3 hashtags, at the end. |
| Telegram | 4096 | Channel post. Short paragraphs; a few emoji as bullets are fine. No hashtags. |
| Farcaster | 320 | Crypto-native builder audience on Base. Plain, direct, no hashtags, no hype. |
| Instagram | 2200 | Caption. Links are not clickable: "Thesis link in bio". Up to 5 hashtags at the end. |
| Threads | 500 | Casual and short. One idea per post. |

Formats: single post, thread (X, Threads, Farcaster only), long-form (LinkedIn, Telegram only), announcement, insight / take, story.

## Adapting across platforms

When adapting a post, rewrite it for the target. Do not cut it down mechanically. Keep the angle, the facts and the CTA target. A thread becomes long-form on LinkedIn or Telegram, and a single post elsewhere.

## Platform SEO

- LinkedIn and X are searched in-app. Put the primary keyword (or its natural phrasing) in the first line.
- Hashtags are keywords, not decoration. Use them only from the thesis `keywords` and only within each platform's limit above.
- LinkedIn articles and newsletters are indexed by Google. Long-form thesis explainers can go there with the article template from `seo.md`.
