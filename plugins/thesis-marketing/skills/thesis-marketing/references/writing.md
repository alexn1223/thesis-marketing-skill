# Writing

## Build every piece around the thesis

- The thesis is the anchor. Whatever the topic, connect it to the thesis: a signal that supports it, a question it answers, a company or trend that fits it, a lesson that sharpened it.
- If the topic has nothing to do with the thesis, find the honest bridge in one sentence.
- Show conviction through reasoning and specifics, not hype.
- Write in the first person as the organizer, unless the user asks otherwise.

## Style

- Lead with the most interesting idea. The first line decides if anyone reads the second.
- Concrete beats abstract. Name the trend, the mechanism, the example.
- Short sentences. Cut adjectives that carry no information.
- Sound like a person. Never: "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock".
- Emoji: at most 2 per post, unless the tone is playful or the platform is Telegram.
- No Markdown in social posts. Plain text and line breaks only.
- Voice samples (from `status` → `profile.voice_samples`): match rhythm, vocabulary and formatting habits. Do not copy their content.

## Ideas

Each idea is one line under 90 characters and specific enough to write from: an angle, not a category. Mix the types:

- a contrarian take
- a signal from the market
- an explainer of one key point
- a personal lesson
- a question to the audience
- an answer to one of the thesis `questions`, which doubles as SEO
